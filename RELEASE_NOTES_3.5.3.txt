Space Trainer — single-player trainer for Grand Theft Auto V.

## Space Trainer 3.5.3

### What's new
- **SPACE TRAINER** branding with smoother UI animations (open fade, selection slide, accent pulse)
- **Zombie Apocalypse** — realistic chaos outbreak: random pedestrians and drivers get infected, corpses rise after a delay, armed survivors can fight back, cops can turn too
- **Professions** — Firefighter (fireman model + fire truck + fire calls) and Inkassator / cash collector (armored guard models + Stockade, defend delivery or van heist)
- **Disasters** — Tornado, Flood, Meteor Shower
- **FreeCam fix** — character stays visible while the camera flies freely
- Better bodyguard vehicle entry and follow
- Film camera and Top-Down camera fixes
- Tools Pack: smart vehicle cleanup, film cams, gangs, realistic traffic (Quick Search console removed)
- Compact menu hubs and safer UTF-8 Russian text
- Default language: English (Russian supported)

### From 3.5.2
- Contract Hits (5 mission types) with stealth until aimed/shot
- Rebindable open menu key in Settings
- Safer blackout and safer vehicle spawn
- PedList.xml and SpaceStuff data included

### Install
1. Install ScriptHookV + ASI Loader  
2. Disable BattlEye in Rockstar Launcher  
3. Copy `Space.asi` and the `SpaceStuff` folder into your GTA V directory (same folder as `GTA5.exe`)  
4. Launch Story Mode and press F5  

### Requirements
- GTA V (PC)
- ScriptHookV
- ASI Loader
- Story Mode / Single Player only

**DO NOT USE IN GTA ONLINE.**
