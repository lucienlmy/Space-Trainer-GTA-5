SpaceStuff — data folder for Space Trainer
==========================================

Copy this ENTIRE folder next to GTA5.exe (same place as Space.asi):

  Grand Theft Auto V\
    Space.asi
    SpaceStuff\
      PedList.xml          <- ped categories (Player, Animals, ...)
      PropList.txt
      AnimalRidingData.xml
      PedDecalOverlays.xml
      spaceConfig.ini
      ...

Without PedList.xml, Spawn Bodyguard categories (Player, Animals, etc.)
and Model Changer lists stay empty. Quick Spawn presets still work.

Empty subfolders (Vehicle, Outfit, Spooner, Language, Themes, ...) are
normal — they hold your saved vehicles, outfits, spooner maps, etc.
